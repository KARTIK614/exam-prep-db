### Q4. Identify the chronology of the following battles:
(1) Battle of Haldighati (हल्दीघाटी)
(2) Battle of Khanwa (खानवा)
(3) Second Battle of Tarain (तराइन का द्वितीय युद्ध)

(A) 2, 3, 1 (B) 2, 1, 3
(C) 3, 2, 1 (D) 1, 3, 2

**Answer: ✅ (C) 3, 2, 1**

**Explanation:** Correct chronological order:
- **Second Battle of Tarain — 1192 AD**: Muhammad Ghori defeated Prithviraj III Chauhan
- **Battle of Khanwa — 1527 AD**: Babur defeated Rana Sanga of Mewar
- **Battle of Haldighati — 1576 AD**: Maharana Pratap vs Man Singh (Akbar's forces)

So chronological order: 3 → 2 → 1, which corresponds to option (C).

### Q6. Haveli painting belongs to which century?
(A) 17th century (B) 20th century
(C) 18th century (D) 19th century

**Answer: ✅ (D) 19th century / 19वीं शताब्दी**

**Explanation:** Haveli painting (हवेली चित्रकला) flourished in the **19th century** in Rajasthan. This style developed in the Shekhawati region (Jhunjhunu, Sikar, Churu) where wealthy Marwari merchants decorated their havelis (mansions) with fresco paintings depicting mythological themes, historical events, daily life, and European influences. The havelis of Shekhawati are famous open-air art galleries.

### Q9. Late Hisamuddin was an accomplished artist of which art/craft?
(A) Thewa craft (B) Usta craft
(C) Latt Patti craft (D) Meenakari

**Answer: ✅ (B) Usta craft / उस्ता कला**

**Explanation:** Late **Hisamuddin** (स्व. हिसामुद्दीन) was a master artist of **Usta craft** (उस्ता कला). Usta art is a unique form of miniature painting done on camel leather, wood, and marble — primarily practiced in Bikaner. It involves intricate gold embossing/embroidery work (Manoti/Naqqashi). Hisamuddin was a renowned national award-winning Usta artist who contributed significantly to preserving this Bikaner-specific art form.

**Answer: ✅ (B) Badhar / बधार**

### Q12. Which scholar made a scientific division of Rajasthan's painting style in his book "Rajput painting"?
(A) Dr. Shridhar Andhare
(B) Ananda Coomaraswamy
(C) Dr. Faiyaz Ali
(D) Eric Dickenson

**Answer: ✅ (B) Ananda Coomaraswamy / आनंद कुमार स्वामी**

**Explanation:** **Ananda K. Coomaraswamy** (आनंद कुमारस्वामी), the renowned Ceylonese-Indian art historian, made a scientific classification of Rajasthan's painting styles in his seminal book **"Rajput Painting"** (1916). He was the first to systematically distinguish Rajput painting from Mughal painting and classify the various schools — Mewar, Marwar, Bundi, Kishangarh, etc. He is considered the father of Indian art historical studies.
