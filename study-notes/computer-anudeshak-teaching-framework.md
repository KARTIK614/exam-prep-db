## H.2 Negative Marking Elimination Module

```
STRATEGY SESSION — "When to Attempt, When to Skip"

CONFIDENCE LEVEL │ ELIMINATED │ ACTION
─────────────────┼────────────┼─────────────────────
100% Sure        │ 4 options  │ ATTEMPT (always)
75% Sure         │ 3 options  │ ATTEMPT (EV positive)
                 │   (elim 1) │   100%×1 + 0%×(-0.33) = +1.0
                 │            │   vs 75%×1 + 25%×(-0.33) = +0.67
50% Sure         │ 2 options  │ ATTEMPT (slightly positive)
                 │   (elim 2) │   50%×1 + 50%×(-0.33) = +0.33
25% Sure         │ 1 option   │ SKIP (negative EV)
                 │   (elim 3) │   25%×1 + 75%×(-0.33) = 0.0
0% (Pure Guess)  │ 0 options  │ NEVER ATTEMPT
                 │   (elim 0) │   25%×1 + 75%×(-0.33) = -0.0025

RULE: Attempt only if ≥2 options eliminated
TARGET: Attempt 85-90 Qs out of 100 with ≥75% accuracy = 60-65 marks
```

# PART K: SENIOR TEACHER's NOTES — 15 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE

## K.1 What Actually Works

1. **"MCQ First, Theory Later"** — For Paper II technical topics, start each topic with 5 MCQs. Let students see what they don't know. Curiosity drives learning. Then teach the theory. Then retake the same 5 MCQs. The improvement builds confidence.

2. **"The 80-20 Rule for Syllabus"** — 80% of exam questions come from 20% of the syllabus. Identify that 20% for each subject. In Rajasthan Geography: Aravalli + Rivers + Minerals + IGNP = 20% of content, 80% of questions. Teach these with triple depth.

3. **"Error Notebook > All Notes"** — Every student maintains one notebook only for WRONG answers. Each entry: Question → My Wrong Answer → Correct Answer → Why I Got It Wrong → Memory Hook. This notebook is gold in the last 7 days.

4. **"Peer Teaching for Retention"** — In the last 4 weeks, assign each student ONE topic to teach to 5 classmates. Teaching forces mastery. The student who teaches "Rana Kumbha" will never forget a single fact about him.

5. **"Mock Test Simulation MUST Be Real"** — Same time slot (9 AM), same OMR sheet, same pen, no phone, no water break. The brain encodes context. Realistic simulation = lower exam anxiety.

6. **"Sleep Is a Study Strategy"** — Students who sleep 7+ hours score 15-20 marks higher. Enforce this. No all-nighters allowed. The syllabus is designed to finish at 9 PM. After that, recovery.

7. **"The First 5 Questions Decide the Exam"** — Train students to start with their STRONGEST 5 questions in any paper. Success momentum carries through. Never let them start with Question 1 if it's hard. Scan, pick, solve, then flow.

## K.2 What NOT to Do (Common Student Mistakes)

| MISTAKE | WHY IT FAILS | CORRECTION |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Reading theory books cover-to-cover without solving MCQs | Passive reading = 10% retention | Solve 10 MCQs per topic BEFORE reading theory |
| Making beautiful notes instead of solving questions | Note-making feels productive but isn't | 80% time on questions, 20% on notes |
| Avoiding weak subjects | 100% in strong subject + 40% in weak = fail | Attack weak subjects FIRST every day |
| Over-attempting in negative marking | 90 attempts with 60% accuracy = 54-30(-10) = 44 | 75 attempts with 85% accuracy = 64-4(-1.3) = 62 |
| Changing OMR answers at last minute | First instinct is usually correct | Only change if 100% sure of error |
| Studying new topics in last week | New information pushes out old | Last week = ONLY revision of known topics |
| Comparing with other students | Every student has different strengths | Compete only with your own last mock score |

# PART L: SAMPLE LECTURE SCRIPT — "The Maharana Pratap Session"

*This is how we teach a 2-hour session on the most important single ruler in Rajasthan history:*

```
[0-5 min] WARM-UP QUIZ (5 MCQs on screen):

Q4. What was the name of Pratap's horse? (Chetak)
Q5. Where did Pratap establish his last capital? (Chavand)

"Imagine you're a king who has lost his kingdom. Your capital is occupied.
Your enemy offers you peace with conditions. Every other king has surrendered.
You have no major army, no treasury, living in forests.
What do you do?
This is the story of Maharana Pratap — the man who chose 25 years of
guerrilla warfare over submission. The man Akbar could never defeat."

[10-50 min] MAIN CONTENT (Visual Slides):
├── Slide: Family Tree (Sisodia Line: Bappa Rawal → ... → Udai Singh II → Pratap)

│   ├── Forces: Pratap (3,000 cavalry, 400 Bhil archers) vs Man Singh (10,000+ Mughals)
```
