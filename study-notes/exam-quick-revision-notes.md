### 3.2 "Not Correctly Matched / सुमेलित नहीं" — Common Trick Questions
- Mixing up Guru Shikhar with Ser district (both in Sirohi — tricky)
- Confusing Chambal origin with Banas origin
- Swapping Sambhar lake districts (Jaipur-Nagaur, not Jaipur-Ajmer)
- Mixing Ranthambhore (Sawai Madhopur) with Sariska (Alwar)
- Confusing Baneshwar (Dungarpur — tribal) with Pushkar (Ajmer — cattle)

### 3.3 Chronology (कालक्रम) — Frequently Asked
1. **Unification stages of Rajasthan:** 1. Matsya Union → 2. Rajasthan Union → 3. United State of Rajasthan → 4. Greater Rajasthan → 5. United State of Matsya → 6. Part-B State → 7. Full State Reorganization
2. **Rajput dynasties chronologically:** Pratihara → Chauhan → Guhilot/Sisodia → Rathore → Kachhwaha
3. **Battles:** Tarain I (1191) → Tarain II (1192) → Khanwa (1527) → Haldi Ghati (1576)
4. **Mewar rulers:** Bappa Rawal → Rana Kumbha → Rana Sanga → Udai Singh II → Maharana Pratap

## 4. Exam-Taking Quick Tips

### MCQ Strategy (3-Step Filter)
1. **Elimination first** — Cross out 2 definitely wrong options; now you have 50% chance
2. **Fact-check** — Verify key fact from remaining options against your memory
3. **Negative marking awareness** — If still unsure, skip (don't guess blindly)

### Common Trick Areas
- **Years:** Establishment years frequently changed in options (1857 vs 1858, 1948 vs 1949)
- **Districts:** Mineral/resource location always mixed up between nearby districts
- **Peaks:** Heights of Aravalli peaks interchanged in options
- **Tributaries:** Left-bank vs right-bank tributaries swapped

### Time Management (Prelims)
- Attempt easy/factual questions first (30-40 min)
- Then medium difficulty (40 min)
- Tough/statement-based questions last (40 min)
- 30 min buffer for OMR filling & review
