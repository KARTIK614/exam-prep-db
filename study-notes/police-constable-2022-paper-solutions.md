### Q1. किसी विषय से संबंधित कठिन अवधारणाओं को समझने में विद्यार्थियों की समस्या का समाधान करने के लिए एक शिक्षक को निम्न में से क्या करना चाहिए?

*To solve students' problems in understanding difficult concepts related to a subject, a teacher should:*

(A) Case Study (केस स्टडी)  
(B) Lecturing (व्याख्यान देना)  
(C) Interaction Analysis (अन्तः क्रिया विश्लेषण)  
(D) Fundamental Research (आधारभूत अनुसंधान)

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) Interaction Analysis (अन्तः क्रिया विश्लेषण)**

**📖 Explanation:** When students struggle with difficult concepts, the teacher should analyze the interaction patterns between teacher-student and student-content. Interaction Analysis (Flanders Interaction Analysis) helps identify where communication breaks down in the teaching-learning process. Case studies and lectures are delivery methods, not diagnostic tools. Interaction Analysis allows the teacher to pinpoint exactly where understanding fails and adjust teaching accordingly.

### Q2. विद्यार्थियों में 'Critical thinking' (आलोचनात्मक चिंतन) को बढ़ावा देने के लिए, निम्नलिखित में से कौन सी शिक्षण पद्धति सबसे प्रभावी है?

*To promote Critical Thinking among students, which teaching method is most effective?*

(A) Project-Based Teaching (प्रोजेक्ट आधारित शिक्षण पद्धति)  
(B) Lecture Method (व्याख्यान पद्धति)  
(C) Demonstration Method (प्रदर्शन पद्धति)  
(D) Inductive-Deductive Method (आगमनात्मक-निगमनात्मक पद्धति)

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) Project-Based Teaching (प्रोजेक्ट आधारित शिक्षण पद्धति)**

**📖 Explanation:** Project-Based Learning (PBL) is widely recognized as the most effective method for developing critical thinking. Students actively investigate real-world problems, formulate hypotheses, analyze data, evaluate alternatives, and defend conclusions — all core components of critical thinking. The lecture method is passive; demonstration is teacher-centered; inductive-deductive helps with logic but doesn't provide the holistic problem-solving environment that project-based learning does. John Dewey's "learning by doing" philosophy underpins this approach.

### Q3. आपके दो छात्रों ने कक्षा प्रबंधन में एक समस्या उत्पन्न की है। आप क्या करेंगे?

*Two students have created a problem in classroom management. What will you do?*

(A) Ask them to stand at the back of the class and punish them (उन्हें कक्षा के पीछे खड़े होने और दंडित करने के लिए कहें)  
(B) Continuously scold them and give attention (लगातार उन्हें डांटकर ध्यान दें)  
(C) Give them a little attention (उन पर थोड़ा ध्यान दें)  
(D) Ask them to go out of the class (उन्हें कक्षा से बाहर जाने के लिए कहें)

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) Give them a little attention (उन पर थोड़ा ध्यान दें)**

**📖 Explanation:** This question tests classroom management philosophy. The principle of "Least Intervention" suggests giving minimum attention to disruptive behavior while reinforcing positive behavior. Excessive scolding or punishment (options A, B, D) can escalate behavior issues and damage student-teacher relationships. Brief, calm attention addresses the behavior without making it a power struggle. Sending students out (D) avoids the problem rather than solving it.

### Q4. 'Essay Questions' (निबंधात्मक प्रश्नों) का अनुप्रयोग निम्न में से किसकी जाँच हेतु किया जाता है?

*Essay questions are used to test:*

(A) Application of knowledge in a new situation (किसी नई परिस्थिति में ज्ञान का अनुप्रयोग)  
(B) Communication of ideas (विचारों का संचार)  
(C) All of the above (उपरोक्त सभी)

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) All of the above (उपरोक्त सभी)**

**📖 Explanation:** Essay questions serve multiple assessment purposes simultaneously. They test: (1) Knowledge recall and organization, (2) Application of concepts to novel situations, (3) Critical analysis and synthesis, (4) Communication skills — clarity, coherence, logical flow of ideas, (5) Creativity and original thinking. Unlike MCQs which test recognition, essays test production and construction of responses. Bloom's Taxonomy places essay writing at the higher-order thinking levels (Analyze, Evaluate, Create).

### Q5. सुधा को ठीक से याद है कि उसके पिता का जन्मदिन 18 जून के बाद लेकिन 22 जून से पहले है, जबकि उसकी बहन को याद है कि उनके पिता का जन्मदिन 13 जून के बाद लेकिन 19 जून से पहले है, और उसके बड़े भाई को ठीक से याद है कि उनके पिता का जन्मदिन सम संख्या वाली तारीख को है। जून में सुधा के पिता का जन्मदिन किस तारीख को है?

*Sudha remembers her father's birthday is after 18 June but before 22 June. Her sister remembers it's after 13 June but before 19 June. Her brother remembers the date is an even number. What is the father's birthday in June?*

(A) 16  
(B) 12  
(C) 14  
(D) Insufficient information (अपर्याप्त जानकारी)

**✅ Correct Answer: (D) Insufficient information (अपर्याप्त जानकारी)**

**📖 Explanation:** Let's analyze:
- Sudha: birthday is on 19, 20, or 21 (after 18, before 22)
- Sister: birthday is on 14, 15, 16, 17, or 18 (after 13, before 19)
- Intersection of Sudha AND Sister: **empty set** — there is NO date that satisfies both conditions simultaneously (Sudha says after 18 → {19,20,21}; Sister says before 19 → {14,15,16,17,18}. These don't overlap.)
- Since the two memory constraints contradict, there is insufficient information to determine the date. Even though the brother's data (even number) narrows things, it can't resolve the fundamental contradiction.

### Q7. दिए गए कथनों से कौन सा निष्कर्ष निकाला जा सकता है?

**Statements (कथन):**  
(I) Teaching is an art (शिक्षण एक कला है)  
(II) Drawing is also an art (चित्र बनाना भी एक कला है)

**Conclusions (निष्कर्ष):**  
(I) All artists are teachers (सभी कलाकार शिक्षक हैं)  
(II) All artists know drawing (सभी कलाकार चित्र बनाना जानते हैं)

(A) Only Conclusion I follows  
(B) Only Conclusion II follows  
(C) Neither I nor II follows  
(D) Both I and II follow

**✅ Correct Answer: (C) Neither I nor II follows (न तो I और न ही II अनुसरण करता है)**

**📖 Explanation:** This is a syllogism/logical reasoning question. 
- Statement I: Teaching ⊂ Art (Teaching is a SUBSET of Art). "Teaching is an art" means teaching is ONE TYPE of art. It does NOT mean ALL art is teaching.
- Statement II: Drawing ⊂ Art (Drawing is a SUBSET of Art). Same logic — drawing is ONE type of art.
- Conclusion I: "All artists are teachers" → Art ⊂ Teaching. This REVERSES Statement I. Invalid — this is the fallacy of illicit conversion. Just because all teachers are artists (if we grant that) doesn't mean all artists are teachers.
- Conclusion II: "All artists know drawing" → Art ⊂ Drawing. Similarly invalid. Drawing is a type of art, not the other way around.
- Both conclusions commit the logical error of converting "All A are B" to "All B are A," which is invalid.

- Actual color of grass → Green (हरा)

- Therefore: Grass = Red (लाल)

### Q12. निम्नलिखित में से एक को छोड़कर सभी एक निश्चित संबंध साझा करते हैं। उस बेजोड़ को पहचानें:

*Find the odd pair that does NOT share the common relationship:*

(A) Crime : Punishment (अपराध : सजा)  
(B) Judgment : Advocate (निर्णय : अधिवक्ता)  
(C) Enterprise : Success (उद्यम : सफलता)  
(D) Exercise : Health (व्यायाम : स्वास्थ्य)

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) Judgment : Advocate (निर्णय : अधिवक्ता)**

**📖 Explanation:** The relationship in (A), (C), and (D) is **Cause → Effect / Action → Result**:
- Crime leads to Punishment (अपराध → सजा)
- Enterprise/Effort leads to Success (उद्यम → सफलता)
- Exercise leads to Health (व्यायाम → स्वास्थ्य)

In (B): Judgment is NOT caused by an Advocate. An advocate argues a case; a judge delivers judgment. This is an **Actor-Action** relationship, not Cause-Effect. Hence (B) is the odd one out.

### Q13. एक बाइनरी ट्री के पोस्ट ऑर्डर ट्रैवर्सल का पता लगाएं, यदि इसके प्रीऑर्डर और इनऑर्डर ट्रैवर्सल क्रमशः ABCDEF और BADCFE दिए गए हैं।

*Find the Post-Order traversal of a binary tree given Pre-Order = ABCDEF and In-Order = BADCFE.*

(A) BDFECA  
(B) BDEFCA  
(C) BDFCEA  
(D) BEDFCA

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) BDFECA**

**📖 Explanation:** To find Post-Order from Pre-Order and In-Order:

**Step 1 — Construct the tree:**
- Pre-Order: A B C D E F → Root = A (first element in pre-order)
- In-Order: B A D C F E → A splits into Left: {B}, Right: {D, C, F, E}

Left subtree of A: 
- Pre: B → Root = B, In: B → B has no children

Right subtree of A:
- Pre: C D E F → Root = C (first in right subtree pre-order)
- In: D C F E → Left of C: {D}, Right of C: {F, E}

Left subtree of C: D (leaf node)

Right subtree of C:
- Pre: E F → Root = E
- In: F E → Left of E: {F}

Tree structure:
```
        A
       / \
      B   C
         / \
        D   E
           /
          F
```

**Step 2 — Post-Order (Left → Right → Root):** B → D → F → E → C → A = **BDFECA**

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) 10**

### Q92. Assume p and q are non-zero positive integers. What does this code do?
```c
while(p != 0) {
    if(p > q)
        p = p - q;
    else
        q = q - p;
}
printf("%d", q);
```

(A) Subtracts small number from large number  
(B) Computes GCD of the two numbers  
(C) Computes LCM of the two numbers  
(D) The loop will run infinitely

**✅ Correct Answer: (B) Computes GCD of the two numbers**

**📖 Explanation:** This is **Euclid's Algorithm** for finding GCD (Greatest Common Divisor) — specifically the subtraction-based variant. It repeatedly replaces the larger number with the difference until one becomes zero. The other number is the GCD. 
- Example: p=12, q=18 → 12<18 → q=18-12=6 → p=12>6 → p=12-6=6 → p=q=6 → p=0, print q=6. GCD(12,18)=6. ✓
- This is NOT the Euclidean algorithm (which uses modulo). It's the older subtraction-based method.

### Q95. Which statement(s) is/are TRUE about Exception Handling?

### Q100. Which declaration is INCORRECT in Python?

(A) `x,y,z=5,000 6,000 7,000`  
(B) `x_y_z=5,000,6000,7000`  
(C) `x=y=z=5,000,000`  
(D) `x,y,z = 5000, 6000, 7000`

**✅ Correct Answer: (A) `x,y,z=5,000 6,000 7,000`**

**📖 Explanation:**
- **(A) INCORRECT:** `5,000 6,000 7,000` has spaces between numbers instead of commas. Python would interpret this as separate tokens with syntax errors. In Python, tuple assignment needs commas between values: `5,000, 6000, 7000`. The spaces without commas make this invalid syntax.
- **(B) Correct-ish:** `x_y_z=5,000,6000,7000` assigns a tuple `(5, 0, 6000, 7000)` to x_y_z. Valid Python, though probably unintended (5,000 is actually two values: 5 and 0).
- **(C) Correct-ish:** `x=y=z=5,000,000` assigns the tuple `(5, 0, 0)` to all three variables. Valid Python.
- **(D) CORRECT:** `x,y,z = 5000, 6000, 7000` — clean, unambiguous tuple unpacking.

**Note:** Python doesn't support underscores or commas IN number literals (like 5,000). 5,000 is interpreted as a tuple (5, 0), not the number 5000.
