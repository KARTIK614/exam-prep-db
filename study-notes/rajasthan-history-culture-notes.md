### 1.3 Mauryan & Post-Mauryan Period (मौर्य एवं उत्तर-मौर्य काल)
- Rajasthan was part of **Maurya Empire / मौर्य साम्राज्य** under Chandragupta Maurya & Ashoka
- **Ashoka's Bhabru inscription** (Bairath) — only Ashokan inscription in Rajasthan, mentions Buddhist Sangha
- Post-Mauryan: **Kushans / कुषाण**, **Shakas / शक** ruled parts of Rajasthan
- Shaka Kshatrapas / शक क्षत्रप — ruled western Rajasthan; Rudradaman I / रुद्रदामन प्रथम (Junagarh inscription mentions Rajasthan)

## 2. Rajput Period (राजपूत काल) — 7th to 12th Century CE

> ⭐ **HIGHEST WEIGHTAGE SECTION — 8-10 questions per exam**

### 2.1 Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty (गुर्जर-प्रतिहार राजवंश) — c. 730-1036 CE

| Ruler (शासक) | Period | Key Facts |
|--------------|--------|-----------|
| **Nagabhata I / नागभट्ट प्रथम** | c. 730-760 | **Founder** of the dynasty; Defeated Arab invasions (from Sindh) |
| **Vatsaraja / वत्सराज** | c. 780-800 | Title: **Ranahastin / रणहस्तिन**; Tripartite struggle / त्रिपक्षीय संघर्ष began |
| **Nagabhata II / नागभट्ट द्वितीय** | c. 800-833 | Most powerful ruler; Defeated Dharmapala of Bengal; Made **Kannauj / कन्नौज** capital |
| **Mihir Bhoj / मिहिर भोज (Bhoja I)** | c. 836-885 | **GREATEST ruler** — title: **Adivaraha / आदिवराह**; Unified large empire |
| **Mahendrapala I / महेंद्रपाल प्रथम** | c. 885-910 | Extended empire eastward |
| **Mahipala I / महिपाल प्रथम** | c. 913-944 | Empire declined; Indra III of Rashtrakuta sacked Kannauj |
| **Rajyapala / राज्यपाल** | c. 990-1018 | Last notable ruler; Mahmud Ghazni's invasion weakened empire |

**Key Facts:**
- Capital: **Kannauj / कन्नौज** (later period), Ujjain, Bhinmal (early)
- **Bhinmal / भीनमाल** (Jalore, Rajasthan) was an early Pratihara centre
- Famous temple: **Bateshwar Temples / बटेश्वर मंदिर** (Morena) — Pratihara style

## 3. Mughal-Rajput Relations (मुगल-राजपूत संबंध)

> ⭐ **5-6 questions per exam**

### Akbar's Rajput Policy (अकबर की राजपूत नीति)
- **Bharmal / भारमल** of Amber — first Rajput chief to accept Mughal alliance (1562); His daughter married Akbar
- **Man Singh / मान सिंह** (Kachhwaha) — Akbar's most trusted Rajput general, member of **Navaratnas / नवरत्न**
- **Todar Mal / टोडरमल** — Revenue minister; implemented land revenue system / भू-राजस्व व्यवस्था
- Akbar conquered **Chittorgarh (1568)** and **Ranthambhore (1569)**
- **Maharana Pratap** refused to submit; Battle of Haldi Ghati (1576)

### Jahangir (जहाँगीर)
- Mewar submitted via Treaty of Gogunda (1615) — **Amar Singh I** accepted Mughal suzerainty

### Shah Jahan (शाहजहाँ)
- Rajput regiments fought in Mughal campaigns (Kandahar, Balkh, Qandahar)

### Aurangzeb (औरंगज़ेब)
- Conflict with Rajputs due to religious policies:
  - **Jaziya tax / जज़िया कर** reimposed
  - Renewed temple destruction (Vishwanath, Keshava Dev)
- **Rajput War (1679-1681):** Aurangzeb occupied Marwar and Mewar
- **Durgadas Rathore / दुर्गादास राठौड़** — saved infant Ajit Singh, led 25-year guerrilla resistance; eventually, Marwar was restored
- Rajput rebellion was eventually settled but trust was broken permanently

## 10. Modern Rajasthan — Unification (आधुनिक राजस्थान — एकीकरण)

> ⭐⭐⭐ **MOST IMPORTANT — 3-5 questions in every exam**

Rajasthan's unification happened in **7 stages**:

| Stage (चरण) | Date (तिथि) | Name (नाम) | States Included | Capital / Group |
|-------------|------------|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **1st** | 18 March 1948 | **Matsya Union / मत्स्य संघ** | Alwar, Bharatpur, Dholpur, Karauli | Alwar |
| **2nd** | 25 March 1948 | **Rajasthan Union / राजस्थान संघ** (East Rajasthan) | Bundi, Kota, Jhalawar, Dungarpur, Banswara, Pratapgarh, Kishangarh, Tonk, Shahpura | Kota |
| **3rd** | 18 April 1948 | **United State of Rajasthan / संयुक्त राजस्थान** | Udaipur (Mewar) joined Rajasthan Union | Udaipur |
| **4th** | 30 March 1949 | **Greater Rajasthan / वृहद राजस्थान** | Jaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, Jaisalmer joined United State of Rajasthan | Jaipur |
| **5th** | 15 May 1949 | **United State of Matsya / संयुक्त मत्स्य** | Matsya Union merged into Greater Rajasthan | (Merged into Rajasthan) |
| **6th** | 26 January 1950 | Rajasthan came into full effect as a **Part-B state** of Indian Union | Sirohi merged (except Abu-Delwara) | Jaipur |
| **7th** | 1 November 1956 | **Reorganization / पुनर्गठन** — Full State of Rajasthan | Ajmer-Merwara merged; Abu-Delwara region from Bombay; Sunel Tappa from MP; Sironj transferred to MP | Jaipur |

### Key Facts:
- **Sardar Patel / सरदार पटेल** & **V.P. Menon / वी.पी. मेनन** led unification
- **30 March 1949** — considered **Rajasthan Diwas / राजस्थान दिवस** (formation of Greater Rajasthan)
- **First Chief Minister:** Heera Lal Shastri / हीरा लाल शास्त्री (appointed 7 April 1949)
- **First Governor (Rajpramukh):** Maharaja Sawai Man Singh II of Jaipur
- Last princely state to merge: **Sirohi / सिरोही**
