## 1. Rajasthan Polity & Administration (राजस्थान की राजव्यवस्था एवं प्रशासन)

### 1.1 State Governance Structure

| Office / Body (पद/निकाय) | Details |
|---------------------------|---------|
| **Governor / राज्यपाल** | Constitutional head of state; Appointed by President of India; Executive powers exercised on advice of CM & Council of Ministers |
| **Chief Minister / मुख्यमंत्री** | Real executive head; Leader of majority party in Legislative Assembly; Appointed by Governor |
| **Council of Ministers / मंत्रिपरिषद** | Includes Cabinet Ministers, Ministers of State — max 15% of Assembly strength |
| **Legislative Assembly / विधान सभा** | Unicameral; Currently 200 seats; Maximum term 5 years |
| **High Court / उच्च न्यायालय** | Principal seat at **Jodhpur / जोधपुर**; Bench at **Jaipur / जयपुर** |

### 1.4 Panchayati Raj System (पंचायती राज व्यवस्था)

> ⭐ **HIGH YIELD — Exam favourite topic**

Rajasthan was the **first state** to implement Panchayati Raj (Three-Tier System) after the **Balwant Rai Mehta Committee (1957)** recommendations.

**Inauguration:** **2 October 1959** by PM **Jawaharlal Nehru** at **Nagaur / नागौर**

| Tier (स्तर) | Body | Head |
|-------------|------|------|
| **Village Level / ग्राम स्तर** | Gram Panchayat / ग्राम पंचायत | Sarpanch / सरपंच |
| **Block Level / प्रखंड स्तर** | Panchayat Samiti / पंचायत समिति | Pradhan / प्रधान |
| **District Level / जिला स्तर** | Zila Parishad / जिला परिषद | Zila Pramukh / जिला प्रमुख |

**73rd Constitutional Amendment 1992 (73वाँ संविधान संशोधन):**
- Gave constitutional status to Panchayati Raj
- Added Part IX and Schedule 11 to Constitution
- Rajasthan enacted **Rajasthan Panchayati Raj Act, 1994**
- Reservation: 1/3 seats for women; SC/ST reservation as per population
- State Election Commission conducts Panchayat elections
- Term: 5 years

### 1.5 Urban Local Bodies (नगरीय निकाय)

| Body | Population Criterion |
|------|---------------------|
| **Nagar Nigam / नगर निगम** | Cities with population > 1 lakh |
| **Nagar Parishad / नगर परिषद** | Towns with population 20,000-1 lakh |
| **Nagar Palika / नगर पालिका** | Smaller towns |

**74th Constitutional Amendment 1992 (74वाँ संविधान संशोधन)** — Constitutional status for Urban Local Bodies; Part IXA & Schedule 12

## 5. Rajasthani Language & Literature (राजस्थानी भाषा एवं साहित्य)

### 5.1 Language
- **Rajasthani** is an **Indo-Aryan** language
- Script: **Devanagari / देवनागरी**
- Recognized as a regional language but not included in **8th Schedule** of Constitution (demand for inclusion ongoing)
- Major dialects of Rajasthani:

| Dialect (बोली) | Region (क्षेत्र) |
|----------------|------------------|
| **Marwari / मारवाड़ी** | Jodhpur, Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Barmer, Nagaur, Pali |
| **Mewari / मेवाड़ी** | Udaipur, Rajsamand, Chittorgarh, Bhilwara |
| **Dhundhari / ढूंढाड़ी (Jaipuri)** | Jaipur, Dausa, Tonk |
| **Hadoti / हाड़ौती** | Kota, Bundi, Baran, Jhalawar |
| **Shekhawati / शेखावाटी** | Sikar, Jhunjhunu, Churu |
| **Bagri / बागड़ी** | Sri Ganganagar, Hanumangarh, NW Bikaner |
| **Mewati / मेवाती** | Alwar, Bharatpur (Mewat region) |
| **Wagdi / वागड़ी** | Banswara, Dungarpur |
| **Malvi / मालवी** | Jhalawar, parts of Kota (Malwa influence) |
| **Nimadi / निमाड़ी** | Border areas with MP |

### 5.2 Major Literary Figures of Rajasthan

| Figure | Contribution |
|--------|-------------|
| **Chand Bardoi / चंद बरदाई** | Prithviraj Raso (Hindi literature's first epic / हिन्दी का प्रथम महाकाव्य) |
| **Meera Bai / मीराबाई** | Devotional poetry (Krishna Bhakti) in Braj-Rajasthani |
| **Dadu Dayal / दादू दयाल** | Nirguna Bhakti poetry |
| **Suryamal Mishran / सूर्यमल मिश्रण** | Wrote "Veer Satsai" — about Rajput valour |
| **Vijaydan Detha / विजयदान देथा (Bijji)** | Rajasthani short story writer; "Batan ri Phulwari" |
| **Kanhaiyalal Sethia / कन्हैयालाल सेठिया** | Rajasthani poet; "Dharti Dhoran Ri" |
| **Narayan Singh Bhati / नारायण सिंह भाटी** | Rajasthani folklorist & historian |
| **Shiv Chandra Bhardwaj / शिव चंद्र भारद्वाज** | Freedom fighter & writer |
